§2 · WHY ROLES AREN'T ENOUGH

A role is a standing permission. A grant is one answer.

Keep your database roles and IAM. They decide who may connect. VaultKit decides what a specific question may return, and remembers why it was asked.

database role GRANT SELECT ON payments TO agent_reader; Every column, every row, any time range, until someone revokes it. No record of why.
vaultkit grant payments{id, amount, status, customer_email:masked} where status = failed, last 24h · INC-123 · ttl 2h Bound to this exact query. Approved by Ada and recorded. Masking still applies. Expires on its own, or the moment the policy changes.
